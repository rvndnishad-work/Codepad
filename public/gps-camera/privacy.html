GPS Camera privacy policy

Effective [DATE YOU PUBLISH] · GPS Camera for Android and iPhone, made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]

Your photos and locations stay on your phone. We run no servers and have no accounts, so we never receive your photos, your location or your name.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos. Photos you take are saved on your phone: inside the app, and in your phone's gallery if "Save to phone gallery" is on. The app doesn't upload them. If you share a photo, it goes only where you send it.
  • Location. While the app is open it uses your phone's location to print the place, address, coordinates and a small map on your photos, and to write GPS tags into each photo's EXIF data. Anyone you share a photo with can see that location. The app doesn't use location in the background and keeps no location history.
  • Settings and your logo. Your stamp settings, and the company logo you choose (Pro), are stored on your phone only.

What leaves your phone, and why

The app sends the minimum needed to these services. We don't receive any of it.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers and cookies to show and measure ads and, where you allow it, to personalise them. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), the app asks before any ads load, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google explains its use of data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying GPS Camera Pro removes ads, and the ads SDK is then not started.

Purchases

Google Play or the App Store processes payments. We never see your payment details; the app only learns whether you own GPS Camera Pro.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos.
  • Location, while using the app: for the stamp and EXIF tags, as above.
  • Photos or storage: to save photos to your gallery and, with Pro, to pick a logo image. On recent Android versions the system photo picker gives the app only the image you choose.
  • Internet: for maps, addresses, weather, ads and purchases.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. The app keeps working without location; photos then carry no place or coordinates.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and deleting data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Deleting photos in the app removes them; uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, except copies already in your phone's gallery. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. Requests about data held by Google, MapTiler or MET Norway go to those companies.
